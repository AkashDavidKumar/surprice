Origin of April Fools'

April Fools' Day, celebrated on **April 1st**, is an annual tradition of practical jokes and hoaxes.

Its true origin is a mystery, but many historians believe it dates back to **1582**, when France switched from the Julian to the **Gregorian calendar**. Those who were slow to get the news and continued to celebrate the New Year on April 1st became the butt of jokes and were called **"April Fools."**
